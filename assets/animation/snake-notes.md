# 蛇精素材与规则

参考图：assets/sprites/snake-reference.png。最终素材：snake-poses.png，内置 imagegen 生成，原始输出 exec-757059a7-6618-4fcd-9f29-df7b54b87c5c.png。两个姿势分别为游动待机和如意攻击，保留双环发髻、紫色皮肤、金色如意、黑金鳞片和橙色斑纹。

生成提示：

Create a clean transparent 2D game sprite atlas faithfully based on this Chinese cartoon Snake Queen 蛇精 reference. TWO separate full-body poses in one wide 1536x1024 image, exactly equal halves. Both FACE LEFT. Preserve pale lavender skin, angular long face and narrow teal eyes, tall black double-loop hair bun, gold crown hairpin, blue-gold earrings, ornate gold-orange shoulder armor and bodice, long black serpentine scaled tail with gold scale outlines and orange markings, holds golden ruyi scepter with red gem and orange tassel. Left pose gliding idle with ruyi raised at chest, coiled tail resting on invisible ground; right pose striking/casting with ruyi arm extended left, tail curled differently. Feet/tail base aligned y=940, top hair y=80. Each figure entirely confined to its own equal cell: left within x60..700, right within x830..1470, blank transparent gutter. Body size identical, face/head positions match. Thick clean ink outlines and flat handpainted cel colors matching reference. True transparent alpha outside character; NO shadows, no halos, no glow, no aura, no scenery, no text, no panel borders. All tail and hair must be visible without clipping. Elegant villain, not cute chibi.

规则：1000 生命，速度 10，近身伤害 100/秒。哪吒火焰和穿山甲爆炸每次对蛇精最多造成 500 伤害，满血需要两次；普通弹丸原伤害不变，水弹减速照常。半血不产生青蛙残肢，死亡使用蛇精整身倒地淡出。蛇精仅在 1-10 的第 205 秒登场一次。用户已确认保留哪吒，不新增红孩儿。
