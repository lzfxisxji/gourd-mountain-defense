# 地图预览 v1

生成方式：内置 image_gen。当前为美术方向预览，尚未接入可运行游戏。

## 第一章：木境·葫芦藤园

+Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: finished 2D tower defense game background map concept, landscape 16:9.
Create chapter one map "wood element gourd garden", for a Chinese Calabash Brothers themed Plants vs Zombies gameplay adaptation. Visual language of classic Chinese hand-painted cel animation, decorative ink-brush mountain shapes, flat gouache fills, bold organic outlines, warm nostalgic 1980s Chinese cartoon background painting, charming professional game art.
CRITICAL PLAYFIELD: fixed camera, nearly top-down orthographic ground plane, absolutely straight horizontal lanes, a large unobstructed rectangular lawn occupying x=19%-88%, y=26%-90% of image. EXACTLY FIVE horizontal rows and EXACTLY NINE equal columns, all 45 planting tiles visible. Subtle alternating light jade-green and moss-green grass tiles with softly painted edges, easy to count, no perspective foreshortening, no objects or shadows obscuring tiles. Grid must read as five horizontal combat lanes.
Left x=8%-18% is a narrow safe garden path with five small golden-orange gourds lying horizontally, exactly one aligned to each lane, the lawnmower equivalents. At far left edge a small rustic grandfather cottage and gourd trellis, mostly outside playfield. Right x=89%-100% is open dirt enemy entry path spanning all five lanes, with forest and mountain cave at extreme right margins. Above the field, reserve y=0%-22% as a quiet warm tan bamboo/wood empty horizontal HUD backing with a round empty resource medallion at left and six EMPTY vertical card sockets, no text, no icons. Thin vine trim around HUD, no obstruction.
Scenery only around perimeter: giant gourd leaves, hanging green gourds, bamboo fence, layered blue-green pointed mountains, wisps of white cloud at top perimeter. Warm late morning light. Playfield lighter and lower contrast than perimeter for readable sprites later.
No characters, no enemies, no projectiles, no text, no logos, no watermark, no title. Single full-bleed game map, no mockup device, no split panels. This is map art for stages 1-1 through 1-10.

## 第二章：水境·寒潭溪谷

+Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: finished 2D tower defense game background map concept, landscape 16:9.
Create chapter two map "water element cold pool valley", for a Chinese Calabash Brothers themed Plants vs Zombies gameplay adaptation. Match classic Chinese hand-painted cel animation, decorative ink-brush mountain shapes, flat gouache fills, bold organic outlines, nostalgic 1980s Chinese cartoon background painting, polished charming game art.
CRITICAL PLAYFIELD: fixed camera, nearly top-down orthographic ground plane, straight horizontal lanes. Large unobstructed RECTANGULAR DRY PLANTING FIELD occupying x=20%-87%, y=29%-91%. EXACTLY FIVE rows and EXACTLY NINE equal columns, 45 clear square-ish tiles, alternating soft muted teal-green moss and light blue-grey earthy stone, softly painted grid edges. Field is level solid dry ground, NOT a pool; all five rows support ordinary defenders, no special water gameplay. No perspective foreshortening. No objects obscure the grid.
Left x=12%-19% is a narrow dry stone safe path with EXACTLY FIVE golden-orange gourds lying horizontally, one centered beside each row. Right x=88%-100% is a dry enemy entrance path connecting all five rows, framed at extreme upper right by a dark blue rocky cave.
Reserve top y=5%-22% for an empty bamboo-and-weathered-wood horizontal HUD: a round empty resource medallion followed by six empty tall rectangular card sockets. No lettering, no icons.
Decorate ONLY OUTSIDE the planting field: blue-green craggy mountains at top, narrow silver waterfall at far upper left falling into a turquoise cold pool along extreme left edge, mist, dark mossy rocks, reeds, gourd vines, a tiny stone lantern by bottom perimeter. A thin stream is allowed ONLY along lower outer edge, never crossing the playing field or lanes. Cool moonlit jade/cyan palette but bright readable gameplay ground, gentle cinematic mist. Warm orange gourds contrast with cool surroundings.
No characters, no enemies, no projectiles, no text, no logo, no watermark, no title, no device frame, no split panels. Full bleed single map intended for stages 2-1 to 2-10.

## 后续制作注意

卡槽、格子、可移动葫芦将在游戏中作为独立图层实现；预览中的这些元素目前为背景画面的一部分。正式使用前须清理底图并统一两章战场坐标，避免葫芦移动后留下原图和界面缩放错位。
